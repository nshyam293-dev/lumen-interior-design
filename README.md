## 🧭 Overview

**Lumen** is a concept website prototype designed for a high-end interior design firm[cite: 1]. The visual identity is built around a sophisticated dark-mode aesthetic, warm accent lighting, high-contrast imagery, and minimalist typography to evoke a sense of luxury, calm, and structural elegance.

🔗 **[View Live Interactive Prototype in Figma](https://www.figma.com/proto/w8N6l4hDx3WcAWGd7t1po2/Interior_webpage?node-id=0-1&t=uIs6TQ5ij5xvetLD-1)**

---

## ✨ Key Sections & Features

- **Hero Landing:** Immersive dark ambiance featuring a backlit circular mirror aesthetic, clear value proposition, and prominent conversion triggers (*Get in touch*, *Explore Our Projects*)[cite: 1].
- **Featured Projects Grid:** Curated global architectural and interior showcases, including:
  - **The Loft Studio** *(Berlin, Germany)* — Compact creative workspace balancing focus and clean lines[cite: 1].
  - **Casa Verde** *(Barcelona, Spain)* — Serene Mediterranean villa blending indoor/outdoor living[cite: 1].
  - **Minimal Office** *(Copenhagen, Denmark)* — Clutter-free corporate interior designed for productivity[cite: 1].
- **Disciplines & Services:** Comprehensive breakdowns across **Residential**, **Commercial**, **Hospitality**, **Interior Styling**, and **Consultation & Concept**[cite: 1].
- **Studio Identity:** Grounded with real-world branding details based in Amsterdam, Netherlands[cite: 1].
